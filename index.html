The Product Owner in an AI world

AI changes the craft. It doesn't change who's accountable.

Global KPI harmonization: back to green

Challenge
A hybrid internal/external team on a global initiative, running behind, with unclear ownership.
What I did
Introduced a Stream Lead structure with individual ownership, re‑defined scope and delivery scenarios, built the delivery roadmap, and drove Back‑to‑Yellow and Back‑to‑Green measures. The initiative's status improved.
AI angle: harmonized KPI definitions are what make an AI's “revenue” mean the same thing in every country.
