    The Product Owner in an AI world

    AI changes the craft. It doesn't change who's accountable.

    Every PO will soon have an assistant that drafts stories, summarises steering decks and flags risks. The ones who stand out will be those who know which data can be trusted, which trade‑off is worth making, and how to bring people along. That's where I've spent my career.
